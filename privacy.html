My Mail privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

What My Mail accesses

When you sign in, My Mail asks Google (Gmail API) or Microsoft (Microsoft Graph) for permission to read, organise, label and send email in your account, and, if you allow it, to read email addresses from your phone's contacts to suggest recipients.

Where your data goes

My Mail runs entirely on your phone. Your email, contacts and sign-in tokens go only between your phone and Google or Microsoft. There is no My Mail server: the developer never receives, stores or sees your email, contacts, passwords or tokens.

Sign-in tokens and app settings are stored in My Mail's private storage on your phone. Files you choose to save are written to your phone's Download folder.

What My Mail does not do

  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • No selling, sharing or transferring of your data to anyone.
  • Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine learning models.

Google API Services

My Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Remove an account in My Mail, or uninstall the app, to delete its data from your phone. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or account.live.com/consent/Manage.
